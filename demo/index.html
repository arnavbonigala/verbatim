Verbatim

Lossless speculative decoding for AI agents. Verbatim drafts long spans copied from the agent's own session (file views, diffs, JSON-escaped tool arguments), and the target model verifies each draft in one forward pass. Output is token-for-token identical to normal greedy decoding. Every number on this page was measured on a MacBook Air M3 with llama.cpp and Metal.
